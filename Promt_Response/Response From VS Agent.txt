# MASTER PROJECT PROMPT — EduPath
I want to develop a professional full-stack web application called **EduPath – Student Learning & Career Guidance Platform** using the **MERN Stack**.

## 1. Project Overview
EduPath is a web platform designed mainly for school students, especially students studying for **O/L and A/L**.

The main problem I want to solve is that many students:

- Struggle to find good classes for specific subjects.
- Do not know which teachers/resource persons are suitable for them.
- Have difficulty finding reliable educational resources.
- Are confused about what to do after O/L.
- Are confused about what to do after A/L.
- Do not know about university degree options, professional qualifications, career paths, and alternative education pathways.
- Need guidance from experienced teachers, university students, career advisors, and industry professionals.
The goal of EduPath is to provide a **single platform where students can discover classes, teachers/resource persons, educational resources, and education/career pathways**.

The application should be designed as a real-world product rather than a simple CRUD project.
